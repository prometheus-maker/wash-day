🚗 Should I wash my car today?

The rule: wash when there is no rain today and for the next 2 days (rain chance under 40% and less than 1 mm), and it is above freezing. Forecast from Open-Meteo.

Questions

When is the best day to wash my car?

When no rain is expected that day and the next two days, and it is above freezing. This page checks your local forecast and tells you the first such day.

How long after washing should it stay dry?

At least a day or two, so the car does not get dirty again right away. This page uses three dry days in a row: today and the next two.
